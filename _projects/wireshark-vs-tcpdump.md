## Overview
A Venn diagram comparing Wireshark and tcpdump.

- **Wireshark:** full graphical interface and deep packet inspection.
- **tcpdump:** command-line, lightweight and ideal for servers and scripted captures.
- **Both:** free and open source, save .pcap files, and use the same libpcap/BPF filter syntax.
