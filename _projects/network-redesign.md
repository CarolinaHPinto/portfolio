## Overview
A network redesign for Tempus Fugit Timepieces, a manufacturer with several buildings, a remote data center and a legacy AS/400 system.

## What I did
- Replaced the legacy ring topology with a hybrid star topology for availability and easier management.
- Added dual ISPs behind a central firewall and core switch, plus VPN access to the remote AS/400 server.
- Wrote documentation explaining each change in terms of confidentiality, integrity and availability.
